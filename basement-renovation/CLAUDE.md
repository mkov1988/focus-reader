# Basement renovation repo — read this first

This is a home renovation project, not software. README.md is the map. The
deliverables are docs, mockups, budgets, and shopping/task lists that Michael
executes in the real world.

Standing rules:

- **Dimensions are unverified.** Planning basis is 36 ft × 12 ft × ~7 ft to
  joists, but the real range is 30–40 × 10–15. Never generate cut lists,
  material quantities beyond rough ranges, or orders until Phase 0
  measurements in `docs/existing-conditions.md` are filled in with real
  numbers.
- **Prices are ranges, not quotes.** National-ish US 2026 numbers. Label them
  as estimates everywhere they appear.
- **Photos are ground truth** (`photos/`). If a doc contradicts a photo, the
  photo wins; fix the doc.
- **Don't spend money or book anyone.** No orders, no contractor outreach,
  no permit filings without Michael saying so in the session.
- **Keep mockups in sync.** When a decision lands (tier, layout, wall
  system), update `mockups/index.html` and the docs in the same change.
- **Plain language.** Michael-readable. No renovation jargon without a
  one-line explanation.
- **Direct links only.** When Michael needs to go somewhere, give the exact
  deep link to the final page (e.g. dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens),
  never "go to X, then click Y". He asked for this explicitly (2026-08-30).
- **Scope guard.** Gym / bathroom / laundry / desk nook only. Fridges + food storage are
  moving to the garage and are out of scope. Challenge additions before
  building on them.
- This directory lives temporarily on a branch of `focus-reader` (see
  README "Housekeeping"). Never touch files outside `basement-renovation/`
  on this branch, and never merge this branch into `main`.
