# Options & tradeoffs

The recurring question ("keep the brick and paint it, or finish it?") plus the
other big system choices. Prices are rough US-2026 ranges for ~770 sq ft of
perimeter wall / ~432 sq ft of floor; treat as estimates.

## Walls — the block question

The walls are CMU block (cinder block), not brick. Three real options, and
they mix: most classy-gym builds paint the block in the gym and only frame
where a room needs to feel "finished" (office) or hide plumbing (laundry).

| Option | What it is | Cost (DIY / pro) | Look | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Paint the block** | Clean, masonry primer, 2 coats. Deep color at the gym, warm white elsewhere | $400–800 DIY / $1.5–3k pro | Honest industrial; coursing shows; very "boutique gym" | Do the 48h moisture test first. If walls pass dry: any quality masonry paint. If damp: breathable mineral/silicate paint (or limewash), never a film that traps moisture |
| **2. Skim / parge coat** | Thin cement or plaster coat over block, then paint (or integral-color limewash) | $1–2/sq ft DIY / $3–6/sq ft pro | Softer, monolithic, "old European gym"; hides coursing without losing mass | Labor-heavy; still wants breathable finish |
| **3. Fur + insulate + drywall** | 2×2/2×4 frame off the block, rigid foam or rockwool, drywall | $7–12/sq ft installed | Fully finished house-grade walls; warmer (real R-value), hides wiring | Loses 3–5" per side of an already narrow room; moisture detailing must be right (foam against block, no fiberglass touching masonry) |

**Recommendation:** paint the block in the gym at every tier (it *is* the
look), and only spend on framing where it buys comfort — office walls at Tier
2+, everything at Tier 3. Limewash over block or skim is the sleeper pick for
"classy" at mid cost.

## Ceiling

| Option | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Open joists, painted out one dark color** (airless-sprayed flat black/near-black: joists, ducts, pipes, all of it) | $300–600 DIY (sprayer rental + paint) / $1.5–3k pro | The boutique-gym ceiling. Keeps every inch of height, hides the chaos by unifying it, keeps access to pipes/wires |
| Drop ceiling (grid + tile) | $3–6/sq ft | Loses 3–4" minimum under already-low ducts; reads office-park — against the brief |
| Drywall ceiling | $5–8/sq ft installed | Cleanest look, best for office zone at Tier 3; loses ~1", but access panels needed at valves/junctions |

**Recommendation:** black-out open ceiling in the gym at every tier. Office
ceiling: black-out at T1, drywall at T2–3 (quiet + warm). Laundry keeps
access (open painted, or removable panels) because the DWV plumbing lives there.

## Floors (over the existing slab; verify what the tan surface is first)

| Zone | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gym | 3/4" rubber stall mats (~$650–900) | 8mm rubber rolls, glued (~$1.2–2.2k) | Premium rubber tile or rolls + optional turf lane (~$2.5–4k) |
| Office | Click LVP, oak tone (~$450–750 DIY) | LVP installed (~$800–1.4k) | LVT/engineered + underlayment upgrade (~$1.5–2.5k) |
| Laundry | Keep slab, paint touch-up ($75) | LVP continued (~$400–700) | Porcelain tile + entry mat zone (~$1.5–3k) |

Basement rule: nothing that hates moisture (no solid hardwood, no bare
laminate). Rubber and LVP shrug off both moisture and dropped plates.

## Office/gym divider

| Option | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Blackout curtain on black pipe track, full height | $150–350 | Tier 1. Softens gym noise, disappears when open |
| Steel-look glass partition (aluminum "Crittall-style" kit or local welder) with swing door | $2–4.5k | Tier 2–3. THE classy-gym move — light passes the whole length of the basement, office stays quiet-ish |
| Open shelving / slat divider | $400–1.2k | Alt if glass is out of budget; keeps sightline, zero sound control |
| Framed wall + door | $1.5–2.5k | Most privacy, but chops the long space into a corridor — against the brief |

## Lighting (all tiers, scaled)

- Gym: a warm LED cove strip line where wall meets ceiling, plus 4–6 black
  linear LED bars under the joists, 2700–3500K,
  dimmable. Tier 1 = plug-in versions on the existing circuit; Tier 2+ =
  hardwired on dimmers.
- Office: no overhead glare — wall sconce + desk lamp at 2700–3000K; recessed
  cans only at Tier 3.
- Laundry: bright even task light over counter (under-cabinet strip + ceiling
  fixture).
- Everywhere: swap the bare bulbs for anything with a shade/housing on day 1.

## Comfort & air

- Tier 1: box fan + a good dehumidifier with drain hose ($250–350) — a
  basement gym's real MVP.
- Tier 2+: 12k BTU mini-split at the office/gym boundary ($3–4.5k installed);
  dehumidifier stays.
- Tier 3: tie zones into HVAC properly / second mini-split head, plus an
  ERV/exhaust decision for the sauna option.

## Moisture prep (before any finish, any tier)

1. 48h plastic-sheet test on walls + slab (see existing-conditions checklist).
2. Walk the exterior: gutters, downspout extensions, grade sloping away.
   $0–300 of exterior fixes beats $3k of interior regret.
3. Only then paint/skim/frame. If the test comes back wet, that's a
   stop-and-reassess, not a "paint over it".
