# Inspiration (Michael's reference images, Aug 2026)

Three images in `inspiration/`. They agree with each other, which makes them a
brief. This is the visual target; the 2026-08-29 palette pivot in
`design-brief.md` comes from here.

## The images

**01-halo-mirrors-cove.jpg** — warm greige/taupe walls, near-black ceiling,
two big round backlit mirrors with warm halo glow, a continuous warm LED
line where the wall meets the ceiling, black + brass dumbbells on vertical
towers, black bench with champagne hardware, textured neutral carpet-tile
floor, dark paneled doors. Mood: warm minimal luxe.

**02-black-light-bars.jpg** — black-on-black room: charcoal walls, black
ceiling, rhythmic vertical LED bars running up the walls, full-height mirror
panels, black rack + bench + dumbbells, dark rubber floor, framed monochrome
art, and green plants as the only color. Mood: moody, graphic, gym-first.

**03-wood-ceiling-luxe.jpg** — wood-slat/travertine ceiling with integrated
warm linear light channels and black track bars, dark bronze walls, backlit
mirror and glowing slat panels, one large dramatic B&W artwork, light stone
floor with an area rug. Mood: hotel-spa luxe.

## The extracted rules

1. **Light is the architecture.** Every image leads with engineered light:
   a cove/perimeter LED line, halo-backlit mirrors, vertical light bars,
   glowing ceiling channels. Fixtures are invisible; glow is the feature.
   All warm white (~2700K), always on dark surfaces.
2. **Palette is warm dark neutrals, not green.** Charcoal, espresso, bronze,
   taupe/greige, black. The earlier deep-green direction is retired; green
   now enters only as plants (one per zone).
3. **Black steel + brass/champagne** on equipment and hardware.
4. **Wood carries the warmth** — slat ceiling or wall, oak/walnut.
5. **Dark ceilings everywhere.**
6. **Styling is sparse:** monochrome art, plants, nothing motivational.
