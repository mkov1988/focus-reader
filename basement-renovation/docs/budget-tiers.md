# Budget tiers

Three coherent builds, same palette and layout, rising finish level. All
numbers are **rough US-2026 estimates** (labor varies hard by region), planning
basis 36'×12'. Every tier assumes the moisture test passed and the garage
move (fridges, coolers, pantry shelving, boxes) already happened for free.

Rule across tiers: **spend on the shell in tier order** — a Tier 1 weekend of
paint is never wasted money if Tier 2 comes later; the tiers are designed to
stack.

## What actually drives cost

1. **Labor vs DIY** — Tier 2's range is mostly "who hangs drywall".
2. **The bathroom's below-slab plumbing** — gravity drain vs sewage ejector
   pump is a $2–4k swing; sewer depth (Phase 0) settles it.
3. **Ceiling treatment** — black-out spray is 10× cheaper than drywall
   overhead and arguably more on-brief in the gym.
4. **Surprises** — moisture remediation, panel upgrades, low ducts. That's
   what Phase 0 de-risks.
